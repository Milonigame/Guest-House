Fridges, a cooker, a microwave, a hot-water dispenser and cookware. Each room has its own table by the window.

Shops in the same building: a grocery, a hairdresser and souvenirs.

Bagripsh · Kholodnaya Rechka

A greener side of Abkhazia

The village sits on a slope of the Gagra Range, surrounded by subtropical greenery and cypresses. Magnolias bloom in spring and summer; autumn brings roadside stalls selling mandarins and persimmons.
