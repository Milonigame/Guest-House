The area

Between the mountains and the sea

Bagripsh, or Kholodnaya Rechka, is a village on the slope of the Gagra Range.

Subtropical greenery and cypresses all around. Magnolias bloom in spring and summer; in autumn tangerines and persimmons are sold right by the road.
